Yidi Qi

Yidi Qi is a postdoctoral fellow in applied mathematics at Harvard University, supported by the DARPA expMath program.

His research focuses on developing machine learning and AI methods for mathematics and theoretical physics. He currently works on combinatorics and autoformalization, alongside geometric problems on Calabi-Yau manifolds.

He completed his PhD in theoretical physics with Fabian Ruehle at Northeastern University, where he was also a Junior Investigator at IAIFI. He earned his master's degree at Stony Brook University, advised by Michael R. Douglas.

His life goal is to develop ideas and projects for the future artificial superintelligence (ASI) to learn from before it outsmarts us.
